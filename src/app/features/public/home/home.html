The idea

Your balance isn’t your spendable money

Most apps stop at “what’s in the bank”. We keep going, until the number you see is one you can safely act on.

1 · Liquid cashBank + cash

Only money you can move today. Wallets and emergency savings are treated separately.

−
2 · Card debtBilled + unbilled

Unpaid statements and fresh swipes, so a full card never looks like free money.

−
3 · CommitmentsEMIs & bills

Recurring payments and subscriptions due in your window, paid by cash or card.

=
4 · Real availableTruly free

What’s left once everything owed is set aside.

→
5 · Safe to spendWith a buffer

A cautious figure for day-to-day decisions.

Everything in one place

Built for real money, not just expenses

Cards with statements, loans with EMIs, UPI wallets, shared trips — the messy parts of Indian personal finance, handled properly.

Credit cards that make sense

Record statements with interest, GST and charges, track minimum due and due dates, pay from any account, and see cashback and reward points — with every statement and payment in one history.

  • Statement & payment history per card
  • Cashback to wallet or bank, reward-point expiry

Loans & EMIs, split correctly

Each EMI is divided into principal and interest, with an amortisation schedule, next due date and a designated paying account. Delete a payment and everything rolls back cleanly.

  • Amortisation schedule
  • Auto-debit from recurring items

Bulk entry with safety checks

Paste or key in up to 500 rows. A dry run flags duplicates, future dates and overdrafts before anything is saved.

Recurring & subscriptions

Daily to yearly schedules that never drift on month-ends, upcoming-obligation view, pause and resume, and a cancellation link per subscription.

Goals & savings buckets

Set aside part of an account for a goal — the same rupee is never counted twice.

Insights, health score & reports

A financial-health score, nudges such as lifestyle inflation and subscription creep, budget-vs-actual, category analysis, net worth and account statements you can download.

CSV export

Decision journal

Write down why you made a big money decision — and how it turned out.

Privacy & security

Careful with your data

Money data deserves boring, well-tested safeguards.

Per-device sessions

Each device has its own sign-in. A new device needs an email code.

Short-lived access

Rotating tokens, an idle timeout, and sign-out that takes effect immediately.

Your data is yours

Every record is scoped to your account and checked on the server for each request.

Audit trail

Changes to money records are logged so numbers can always be explained.
